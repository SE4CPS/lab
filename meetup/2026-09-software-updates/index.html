The Update Problem

  • Updates ship constantly
  • Changelogs are free text
  • Breaking changes hide
  • Skipping has risks too

What a Changelog Says

  • Features, fixes, breaks
  • SemVer hints: major.minor
  • Not: impact on your stack
  • Not: known CVEs, reports

Evidence Beyond the Log

  • Release notes and tags
  • CVE databases
  • Community reports
  • Your dependency graph

Agentic AI for Updates

  • Ask in plain English
  • Agent gathers evidence
  • Answer cites sources
  • You decide, informed
