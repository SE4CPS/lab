Software Engineering in Industry 4.0 Ecosystems

The second SEIoTE workshop, themed around the maintenance of software and data in interconnected Industry 4.0 environments: long-term stability, security, and sustainability amid evolving firmware, operating systems, libraries, languages, databases, and AI-driven processes.

Venue
Co-located with the 8th International Conference on Emerging Data and Industry (EDI40 2025)

Proceedings

Status
Concluded — second edition of the workshop

Scope

Maintenance of software and data in Industry 4.0 ecosystems

SEIoTE 2025 explored the long-term stability, security, and sustainability of software systems and data assets amid evolving technologies — firmware, operating systems, libraries, programming languages, databases, and AI-driven processes — in interconnected Industry 4.0 environments.

Topics of interest

What the workshop covered

Maintenance & operations

  • Maintenance strategies for software and data in Industry 4.0 ecosystems
  • Automated testing, continuous integration, and deployment in maintenance workflows

Dependencies & security

  • Managing software dependencies, updates, and versioning in interconnected systems
  • Security challenges in maintaining critical software and data

Sustainability & AI

  • Sustainability in software and data management for the future of Industry 4.0
  • AI-driven maintenance solutions for proactive and predictive problem solving
