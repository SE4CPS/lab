Question 19 (Scheduled Backup, Group-Restricted Access)

Full credit needs:
- A real crontab entry (crontab -e) scheduling the backup script on
  weekdays at 6 AM, e.g. 0 6 * * 1-5 /path/to/backup.sh
- Restricting read access to the reports group, e.g. chgrp reports
  /srv/reports plus chmod 750 (or 640 on the files) so only owner and
  group can read, not everyone else

Partial credit: gets the cron schedule right but doesn't address
group-only read access, or restricts permissions without a real
schedule.

No credit: proposes running the backup manually, or opens the folder
to all users instead of restricting it.

Question 20 (Diagnosing a Runaway Process)

Full credit needs three real steps, in a sensible order:
- Identify: ps aux or top to find the process using the most CPU
- Confirm: watch it over a short period (top refreshing, or pgrep
  to confirm the same PID keeps reappearing) rather than assuming a
  one-time spike is the cause
- Stop it safely: kill (a plain, catchable signal) on that specific
  PID, not a blind kill -9 or a reboot, so other services are not
  disrupted

Partial credit: identifies the process correctly but skips
confirming it is the real cause, or jumps straight to a forceful
kill without trying a normal signal first.

No credit: restarts the whole server, or kills processes at random
without identifying the actual one first.
