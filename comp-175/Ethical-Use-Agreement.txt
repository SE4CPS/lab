COMP-175: System Admin and Security -- Fall 2026
Ethical & Responsible Use Agreement


By taking this course, you agree to use every tool and technique
taught here only against systems you own or have explicit written
authorization to test. Using them against any other system, on or
off campus, is an Honor Code violation and may carry civil or
criminal liability (e.g., under the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act).

  - Authorization is required, always.
  - Only course VMs and explicitly sanctioned targets (e.g.,
    scanme.nmap.org) may be used for practice.
  - Never target real, third-party, or campus systems without
    written permission.
  - Violations are both an Honor Code matter and a potential
    legal one.


I have read, understand, and agree to this Ethical & Responsible
Use policy for the duration of COMP-175.
