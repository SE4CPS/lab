PART 2: DIAGRAM QUESTION (19) -- GRADING GUIDE
================================================================

Full credit needs:
- Kali VM and Ubuntu VM shown as distinct boxes
- Their host/hypervisor shown as a distinct box underneath both
- The connection between Kali and Ubuntu labeled SSH
- A labeled chain on the Ubuntu side from systemd (PID 1) down to
  the running service process

Partial credit for a correct process chain with a missing or
mislabeled hypervisor layer. No credit if the SSH connection is
missing or if Kali and Ubuntu are drawn as the same machine.

PART 3: WRITTEN RECOMMENDATION (20) -- GRADING GUIDE
================================================================

Full credit expects:
- Permission bits (owner/group) or an ACL used to separate billing
  staff (read/write) from nurses (read-only), with everyone else
  denied by default
- For the audit-trail follow-up: either a MAC-based control (so DAC,
  and even root, can't silently override access) or a monitoring/
  audit mechanism that logs access changes

Partial credit for a workable permissions scheme that omits the
audit-trail follow-up. No credit for a scheme that grants everyone
the same access, or that gives the uninvolved administrator access
"just in case."
